Compliance

Which OSHA Rules Do These Products Help You Meet?

OSHA’s construction rules are federal, so they apply the same way on job sites in every state, except where a state OSHA plan adds its own requirements.

Stairs at 19 inchesOSHA 1926.1051(a): a stairway or ladder is required at any break in elevation of 19 inches or more. AdjustaStairs™ and Z-Stack™ provide that stair access.
Stair angleOSHA 1926.1052: stairs must sit between 30° and 50° from horizontal, with uniform risers in each flight.
Scaffold strength and inspectionOSHA 1926.451: scaffolds must hold 4 times their maximum intended load and be inspected by a competent person before each shift.
Safety netsOSHA 1926.502(c): personnel safety nets go no more than 30 feet below the work surface. The RB-412 is built for this.

Do OSHA rules change from state to state?

OSHA's construction standards in 29 CFR 1926 apply nationwide. Some states run their own OSHA-approved plans, which must be at least as effective as federal OSHA and can add requirements, so check your state plan for anything extra. See OSHA state plans.
