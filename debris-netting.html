Installation

Easy to Install at the Working Level

  1. Clamp to the slab

    The patent-pending slab bracket fits over the deck edge, on decks from 4″ to 22″ deep.

  2. Tighten with an impact wrench

    Adjust the bracket to the right width with an impact wrench and secure it to the deck.

  3. Extend the arm and hang the net

    The arm extends up to 13 feet and is held by a 3/8″ support cable. The netting attaches at the eyenut on the end of the arm.

Frequently Asked Questions

Debris and Safety Netting FAQ

Answers from ATI Construction Products.

What is the DS-1320 Debris Netting System?

The DS-1320 is a galvanized steel debris netting system from ATI Construction Products that installs at the working level, clamped to the edge of the slab. Its adjustable arm extends 13 feet from the slab edge to catch material falling from the levels above, and it is designed to meet ANSI A10.37.

Is the DS-1320 rated for fall protection?

No. The DS-1320 is built for falling debris and is not rated for fall protection. Where workers need protection from falls, use the RB-412 PSN Personnel Safety Netting System, which is rated for fall protection and compliant with OSHA 1926 and ANSI A10.11-2016.

What slab thicknesses do the netting brackets fit?

Both the DS-1320 and the RB-412 use a patent-pending slab bracket that fits decks from 4 to 22 inches deep. The bracket is adjusted to the slab and tightened with an impact wrench.

Can I rent safety netting, or do I have to buy it?

The RB-412 PSN Personnel Safety Netting System is available to rent or to buy. Call (800) 360-6115 for DS-1320 and custom netting options and pricing for your project.

Can ATI design netting for an unusual building or site?

Yes. ATI designs custom debris and containment netting systems to your site's exact dimensions and load requirements. They can work alongside the DS-1320 and RB-412 or on their own.

ATI offers same-day quotes on most products and ships nationwide from Arvada, Colorado. Call (800) 360-6115 or send the online quote form.
