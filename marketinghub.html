FAQ

Marketing Hub Questions

What is Marketing Hub?

Marketing Hub is an AI-powered marketing platform from ATI Business Essentials. It connects paid ads, social media, and website analytics into one place, and its AI continuously learns your marketing and your business to show what is actually working. It is a separate platform from Leads Plus, though the two can optionally connect for deeper insight.

Who is Marketing Hub built for?

Marketing Hub is built for small businesses that do not have the time to learn marketing but still want to manage it themselves. It includes warm outreach tools to reconnect with past and current leads, along with AI that continuously learns a company’s specific industry to show what is actually working.

How is Marketing Hub different from Leads Plus?

They are two separate platforms that can work together. Leads Plus is a simple CRM replacement: it captures leads, tracks your bid pipeline, and trains Google Ads to find more customers like your best ones. Marketing Hub connects your advertising, social media, and website into one place and uses AI to surface growth recommendations for targeting, messaging, and scaling. Marketing Hub can optionally read Leads Plus data for added insight, but neither platform requires the other.

What channels does Marketing Hub connect?

Marketing Hub unifies paid ads, social media, and website analytics into one view, and automatically tracks changes made across Google Ads, your website, and social channels to measure their real impact on leads and revenue.

Do I need Leads Plus to use Marketing Hub?

No. Marketing Hub works completely on its own. Connecting it to Leads Plus lets its AI factor in your actual lead and bid data for even more informed recommendations, but it is not required.
