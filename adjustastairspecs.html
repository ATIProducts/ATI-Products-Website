Choose the AdjustaStairs™ model for your roof elevation. Call (800) 360-6115 for expert guidance.

One worker positions the base at the building while a second worker goes to the top. No crane or special equipment needed.

AdjustaStairs™ self-levels into place and locks securely for stable, code-compliant access.

FAQ

Technical Questions Answered

What materials is AdjustaStairs™ made from?

AdjustaStairs™ uses lightweight aircraft-grade aluminum that is durable for daily job site use while light enough for two workers to carry and position. ATI Construction Products engineers every system to balance job site demands with OSHA safety requirements.

How many workers are needed to install AdjustaStairs™?

AdjustaStairs™ typically takes two workers to set up: one positioned at the base and one at the top. The system self-levels into place and locks securely. No tools are required, making it fast to deploy and redeploy across multiple project locations.

Can AdjustaStairs™ be reused across multiple job sites?

Yes. AdjustaStairs™ is a portable temporary system designed to move with the crew from project to project. Its adjustable height adapts to different roof elevations and site conditions across all project types: commercial, residential, and industrial.

How does AdjustaStairs™ compare to a ladder?

OSHA 1926.1051 requires stairways when elevation changes exceed 19 inches and the task requires frequent access. AdjustaStairs™ provides a safer, OSHA-compliant alternative that reduces fall risk, allows workers to carry materials safely, and improves site productivity.

What height range does AdjustaStairs™ cover?

Standard AdjustaStairs™ (660 lb rating) cover 3 to 16 feet in 6 to 20-step configurations. High-Access AdjustaStairs™ with Support Truss (1,100 lb rating) cover 11 to 32 feet in 22 to 40-step configurations. All models are available in 24-inch, 36-inch, or 48-inch widths.
