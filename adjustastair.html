Frequently Asked Questions

AdjustaStairs™ FAQ

Everything you need to know about AdjustaStairs™ from ATI Construction Products.

What is the AdjustaStairs™ system?

AdjustaStairs™ is an adjustable aluminum temporary stair system available through ATI Construction Products, LLC. It provides safe, OSHA 1926.1051-compliant access to roofs and elevated areas on construction sites. It comes in configurations to fit different roof pitches and heights, deploys quickly, and typically takes two workers to set up.

Is AdjustaStairs™ OSHA compliant?

Yes. AdjustaStairs™ is engineered to meet OSHA 1926.1051 standards for stairways on construction sites. It provides a safer alternative to ladders for workers who need frequent roof access, and meets the OSHA requirement for stairways when elevation changes are 19 inches or greater.

What heights does AdjustaStairs™ accommodate?

AdjustaStairs™ is available in two model families: Standard AdjustaStairs™ (660 lb weight rating) in 4 to 20-step configurations covering 2 to 16 feet, and High-Access AdjustaStairs™ with Support Truss (1,100 lb weight rating) in 22 to 40-step configurations covering 11 to 32 feet. All models are available in 24-inch, 36-inch, or 48-inch widths. Call (800) 360-6115 to find the right configuration for your project.

How quickly can I get an AdjustaStairs™ quote?

ATI Construction Products offers same-day quotes on AdjustaStairs™ systems. Contact us before 2 PM Mountain Time at (800) 360-6115 or fill out the online form and we will respond the same business day. We ship nationwide from California.

Does AdjustaStairs™ ship nationwide?

Yes. ATI Construction Products ships AdjustaStairs™ systems to all 50 states from our California facility. We have served general contractors, roofing companies, and construction crews across the country since 1999.

What is the difference between AdjustaStairs™, AdaptaStep™, AdaptaSpan™, and Z-Stack™?

AdjustaStairs™ is a stair tower system for roof access. AdaptaStep™ is a modular access platform system configurable into crossovers, catwalks, platforms, and mezzanines. AdaptaSpan™ bridges horizontal gaps. Z-Stack™ is a modular, craneable stair tower for permanent or long-term vertical access. Contact ATI for a comparison based on your specific project needs.
