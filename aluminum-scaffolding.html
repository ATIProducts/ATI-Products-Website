Aluminum Scaffolding

What Is a Mini Mobile Scaffold Tower?

Mini Mobile towers are rolling aluminum scaffolds from ATI Construction Products. The frames have rungs built in, the platform locks into the frame, and the towers clip together quickly with minimal bracing, so one person can erect one.

There are two series: a 5' 7" single width tower that fits through standard doors, and a 10 ft long series that covers long walls as fast as trestles but is easier to move.

  • ✓500 lb weight rating
  • ✓Complies with ANSI A10.8-2011
  • ✓Platforms from 3 ft to 12 ft, reach heights up to 18 ft
  • ✓Add-ons for stairs, long and wide decks, corners and roof edges

Add-Ons

What Add-Ons Are Available?

Packs extend a Mini Mobile tower for stairs, long walls, ceilings, corners and roof edges.

All Terrain Stair Pack

A diagonal brace and adjustable end frame with 48" of adjustment, for stairs, slopes and uneven ground. Two 10" adjustable casters are recommended. MAT170 (6 ft series), MAT300 (10 ft series), MAC220 casters.

Long Board Extension Set

Joins two towers end to end with a longboard, handrail and posts, spanning 10 to 20 ft over obstacles. MLP300, MLP400, MLP500 and MLP600.

Wide Board Extension Set

Joins two towers side to side to triple the work surface, which suits ceiling work. MWP170 and MWP300.

Corner Extension Set

Lets decking run around left and right corners. Attaches to the side of a 6 ft or 10 ft tower. MCP170 and MCP300.

Saferoof Roof Edge Pack

Frames, eaves brackets and swivel top plates that give access to the roof and walls at once while providing roof edge protection. MMSRP200, with MGR250 and MGR253 side guardrails.

Basic Components

Standard and adjustable casters, end frames, cross braces, access platforms, toeboards, outriggers, guardrails, locking spigots and gravity latches.

Safety

What Safety Rules Apply to Mobile Scaffold Towers?

Mini Mobile towers comply with ANSI A10.8-2011, the American National Standard for scaffolding safety. On construction sites, OSHA 1926.452(w) sets the rules for mobile scaffolds, including locking casters and keeping the tower level.

  • ✓The spec sheets call for outriggers on platforms over 6' 6" high.
  • ✓Toeboards are required on platforms 9' 10" or higher, and some sites require them at any height.
  • ✓Each deck is rated for 500 lbs. Do not overload the platform.
  • ✓For roof work, the Saferoof pack adds edge protection. See OSHA stairway requirements for access between levels on a job site.
Frequently Asked Questions

Aluminum Scaffold Tower FAQ

Answers about Mini Mobile towers from ATI Construction Products.

How high can a Mini Mobile aluminum scaffold reach?

The tallest Mini Mobile towers, the MM410 and MM413, have a 12 ft platform and a reach height of about 18 ft. The smallest, the MM210 and MM213, have a 3 ft platform and reach about 9 ft 5 in. The calculator on this page picks the smallest tower for your height.

What is the difference between the 5' 7" and 10 ft series?

The 5' 7" single width series is 67 inches long and 28 inches wide, so the MM210 fits through standard doors and works well indoors. The 10 ft long series is 120 inches long and 28 inches wide for covering long walls quickly. The 10 ft series adds a 9 ft platform model, the MM313.

How much weight can a Mini Mobile scaffold hold?

Mini Mobile towers have a 500 lb weight rating, and the Saferoof system allows 500 lbs on every deck.

Can one person set up a Mini Mobile scaffold?

Yes. The towers are designed to be erected by one person, with rungs built into the frames and minimal bracing. The 10 ft series clips together up to 12 ft high in about 4 minutes, according to the spec sheets.

Can a mobile scaffold be used on stairs?

Yes, with the All Terrain Stair Pack. It adds a diagonal brace and adjustable end frame with 48 inches of adjustment for stairs, slopes and uneven ground. Two 10 inch adjustable casters are recommended and are ordered separately.

Does ATI sell used scaffolding?

New Mini Mobile towers come from ATI Construction Products, which ships nationwide. Used and surplus scaffolding is bought and sold on Scaffold Exchange.
