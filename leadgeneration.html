FAQ

Leads Plus Questions Answered

What is Leads Plus?

Leads Plus is ATI Business Essentials' simple CRM replacement for service businesses and contractors. It captures every lead, keeps them moving through a bid and job pipeline, and sends your best leads back to Google Ads to train it to find more customers like them. That's all it does, on purpose.

Does Leads Plus work with Marketing Hub?

Yes, but they are separate platforms. Leads Plus works completely on its own as a lead and bid pipeline. Marketing Hub, a separate platform from ATI Business Essentials, connects your social media, paid ads, and website into one place and can optionally read your Leads Plus data for deeper insight. Neither one requires the other.

How does Leads Plus track leads?

Call tracking, form capture, and chat integration record every lead from every source. Leads are tagged by source, quality, and status, and flow into your bid pipeline for full revenue attribution.

Do I still need a separate CRM?

No. Leads Plus is built to replace your CRM for lead and pipeline management. Every lead, bid, and job is captured, qualified, and tracked in one simple dashboard, so there is no separate CRM to buy, learn, or maintain.
