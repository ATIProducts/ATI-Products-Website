Modular Stair Towers

What Is a Z-Stack™ Stair Tower?

Z-Stack™ is a modular, pre-engineered stair tower made by SafeSmart and supplied by ATI Construction Products. Each module is a welded aluminum frame with two flights of stairs and a landing. Modules bolt together and stack on top of each other, so one system can reach a single roof edge or climb with a building as it goes up. SafeSmart built it as a faster, more stable alternative to scaffold stair towers, ladders and cage ladders.

  • ✓Two sizes: Standard with 36" stairs and Compact with 24" stairs
  • ✓Bolt-together modules stack to the height you need, one 8' 8 3/4" Standard module at a time
  • ✓Craneable: lift single modules, or the whole tower with lifting bars
  • ✓Guardrails, landings, toeboards and anti-slip treads built in
  • ✓Reusable aluminum that flat-packs for transport, or stays as permanent access

What Is the Difference Between Standard and Compact Z-Stack™?

Both sizes use the same bolt-together module system. Standard towers have wider stairs for crews moving tools and materials. Compact towers fit where space is tight and work as a true ladder replacement for roof and equipment access.

How It Assembles

How Do Z-Stack™ Modules Stack Together?

Every tower uses three module types: one base module, as many middle modules as the height needs, and one top module. Each module is lifted into place and lowered onto four joiners that stick up from the module below. The male-to-female joiners seat the module, and the supplied bolts and locknuts lock it into one rigid tower.

  1. Set the base module

    Four height-adjustable base jacks anchor the tower to an approved surface and level it on uneven ground. Castors and a mobile chassis are available for indoor sites.

  2. Stack middle modules

    Repeat middle modules to gain height. Each one adds two stair flights and a landing. Tie the tower to the structure with a tie-off bracket every third module.

  3. Finish with the top module

    The top module carries the top handrail and gate. Add joiners and a lifting bar there if the whole tower will be craned.

Craneable

Can a Z-Stack™ Tower Be Moved by Crane?

Yes. Because every connection is fully bolted and locknut-secured, a Z-Stack™ tower holds its shape when it is lifted. The joiners on each module double as crane hooks for lifting single modules, and lifting bars spread the load across the tower so it doesn’t twist when you lift the whole thing.

  • ✓Lifting bars are required to lift 4 or more modules as one structure
  • ✓Towers can be pre-assembled off site and set with one crane pick
  • ✓Reposition the tower as the job changes, without taking it apart
  • ✓Telescopic tie-off brackets secure the tower to the structure every third module

Top Landing

What If the Tower Doesn’t Line Up With the Roof?

Modules come in fixed heights, so the top landing rarely matches the roof or platform exactly. SafeSmart’s planning rule: a gap of 9.5" or less needs nothing added. Bigger gaps get a transition, and a gap of 48" or more means adding another module.

9.6" to 24" difference

AdaptaStep™ Bridging

A modular bridging platform that bolts onto the Z-Stack™ and spans to the destination surface. It can run up to 30 feet long and joins AdaptaStep catwalks for rooftop and equipment access.

24" to 48" difference

AdjustaStairs™ Transition

Self-leveling stairs connect the top landing to a higher or lower level, with no unsafe step-downs or site-built steps. See AdjustaStairs™ specs and sizing.

Accessories

Which Accessories Are Available for Z-Stack™?

Add-ons adapt a tower to the site, the people using it and how long it will stay.

  • Lifting Bars

    Lift the whole tower by crane without twisting. Required for lifting 4 or more modules together.

  • Tie-Off Brackets

    Telescopic brackets tie the tower to a nearby structure to reduce sway. Required every third module.

  • Entry Gates

    Control who enters the tower at the base module. Available in aluminum or mesh.

  • Security Mesh

    Panels along the tower help contain tools and debris and stop unauthorized climbing.

  • Mobile Chassis

    Heavy-duty lockable castors let low towers roll to a new spot inside controlled facilities.

  • Removable Cantilever

    Extends the working platform past the tower footprint to reach equipment or structures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Z-Stack™ Stair Tower FAQ

What is a Z-Stack stair tower?

Z-Stack™ is a modular, pre-engineered aluminum stair tower made by SafeSmart and supplied by ATI Construction Products. Bolt-together modules, each with two stair flights and a landing, stack to the height you need and can be craned into place as one tower.

How tall can a Z-Stack tower be?

There is no fixed limit. Middle modules repeat to add height, about 8' 8 3/4" each for Standard and 8' 1 1/2" for Compact, and towers have climbed almost 10 stories on SafeSmart projects. Taller towers need a tie-off bracket every third module.

How many Z-Stack modules do I need for 28 feet?

For 28 feet, a Standard tower uses 3 modules with a top landing at 26' 7", and an AdaptaStep™ bridge covers the last 1' 5". A Compact tower also uses 3 modules, topping out at 24' 10 1/2", with AdjustaStairs™ for the last 3' 1 1/2". Try any height in the Z-Stack height calculator.

What is the difference between Standard and Compact Z-Stack?

Standard modules have 36" stairs and a 9' 4" x 6' 10" footprint, for larger crews carrying tools and materials. Compact modules have 24" stairs and a 7' 7" x 4' 11" footprint, for roof access, ladder replacement and tight sites.

Can a Z-Stack tower be lifted by crane?

Yes. Module joiners double as crane hooks for single modules, and lifting bars let you lift the whole tower as one structure without twisting. Lifting bars are required to lift 4 or more modules together.

How is a Z-Stack tower secured to a building?

Adjustable base jacks anchor and level the base on an approved surface, and telescopic tie-off brackets secure the tower to the nearby structure every third module to reduce sway.

Does a Z-Stack tower meet OSHA stair requirements?

Z-Stack™ is engineered with integrated stairs, landings and guardrails to provide compliant stair access. OSHA requires a stairway or ladder at any break in elevation of 19 inches or more under 1926.1051, and your site's competent person still confirms the setup for each job.

Is Z-Stack for temporary or permanent access?

Both. Contractors reuse Z-Stack™ towers from job to job, and facilities install them as permanent roof and equipment access, often enclosed in security mesh with an entry gate.
